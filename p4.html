Smart Voting and Face Recognition

PythonFace recognitionVoter identification

A modern digital election system that uses face recognition to make voting easier, faster and more secure.

A voter’s identity is verified through facial features, biometrics or an OTP. Their face is turned into digital data and matched with stored records, so only genuine voters can vote.

It reduces human error, increases transparency, supports remote voting and speeds up counting. It is contactless, and strong security measures protect the data.
